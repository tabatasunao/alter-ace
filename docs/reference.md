## Ace frames

The frame is built in the hangar: 25 points over five stats, up to 10 on each. A stat scales linearly from its value with no points to its value with 10.
Multipliers below 1 are better (less time between shots, shorter reloads).

| Stat | No points | 10 points |
| --- | --: | --: |
| Speed | 180 | 310 |
| HP | 170 | 270 |
| Reload | ×1 | ×0.45 |
| Fire rate | ×1 | ×0.8 |
| Dash | 3.4 s | 1.2 s |

| Quickdraw | 1 | 5 | 10 | 9 | 0 |
| Vanguard | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |

## Armies

- **Allies:** 100 units in 3 groups on the west side. No reinforcements.
- **Enemies:** 300 to 600 (picked in the hangar, steps of 50). Up to 240 deploy in 4 groups in the east.
- **Reinforcements:** the rest arrive from the east edge in battalions of 60, every 25 s or sooner once fewer than 150 enemies remain on the field.
- **Filler class:** Trooper fills whatever the shares leave.

## Tutorial mission

The ace starts alone at (1400, 1700). At each stage start, and when the first mission takes over, the battle shifts in steps of 1000 to bring the ace near the middle of the battlefield. Each stage's enemies deploy east of the ace, 400 beyond the east edge of the view and at least 700 away; the next stage starts 1.2 s after one is cleared. A stage's next wave deploys 1.5 s after the last one is destroyed; once the waves are used up, the group deploys again after the same gap while a goal is open. The ace's HP never drops below 20% of its max HP. 4.5 s after the last stage, the first mission starts without the launch sequence: 30 allies deploy 220 beyond the west edge of the view and 150 enemies beyond the east edge (groups 300 apart), with exactly 1 Commander, 1 Marksman, 1 Circus, 1 Duelist.

Every battle end (victory, defeat, the tutorial's last stage) runs at ×0.3 speed for 1.6 s of real time, after any hit-stop, easing back to full speed over the last 0.4 s. Red edges fade over 0.45 s.
