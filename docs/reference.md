## Armies

- **Allies:** 100 units in 3 groups on the west side. No reinforcements.
- **Enemies:** 300 to 600 (picked in the hangar, steps of 50). Up to 240 deploy in 4 groups in the east.
- **Reinforcements:** the rest arrive from the east edge in battalions of 60, every 25 s or sooner once fewer than 150 enemies remain on the field.
- **Filler class:** Trooper fills whatever the shares leave.
- **Minimums:** 2 Commanders per army, whatever its size.
