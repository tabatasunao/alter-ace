Sortie briefing · Sectors A1 to F4

ALTER

Adaptive Limbframe for Tactical Engagement and Reconnaissance

You fly the ace unit. 100 friendly ALTERs hold the west line while an enemy army of 300 to 600 pushes in from the east, with reserves arriving in waves. Pick a frame, arm four hardpoints and choose how your pilot aims. Bring down the whole army.

Frame

Aim mode

Your pilot fires on their own at the enemy this mode picks inside the active weapon's range. You can switch modes any time in battle.

Up to 240 deploy at the start. The rest arrive from the east in waves of up to 60.

    Controls

    WASD
    Move. Arrow keys work too. On touch, drag anywhere on the field.
    1–4
    Switch to that weapon, or fire that tool. You can also tap the hardpoint.
    QE
    Cycle aim mode. 5–0 picks one directly.
    Shift
    Dash, with a moment of invulnerability.
    Space
    Super release: every weapon hardpoint fires at once.
    R
    Reload the active weapon early.
    +−
    Zoom. The mouse wheel works too.
    P
    Pause. Esc works too.

    Hardpoints 4 / 4

    Pick four. Weapons fire automatically while selected. Tools fire once when you press their key, then recharge. Weapons you aren't using keep reloading in the background.
