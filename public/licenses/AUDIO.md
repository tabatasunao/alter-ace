Every sound effect and music track in `public/audio/` is released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): no rights reserved, no attribution required. The sources are listed for reference. The files were cut, layered, loudness-normalized and converted to MP3; the names in `src/audio/sounds.ts` say what each became.
